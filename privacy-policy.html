Seika

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what the Seika iPhone app ("Seika", "we", "us") collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Seika is made by Ben Schumacher. If you have questions, email benschumacher15@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect what you put into Seika (habits, tasks, brain dumps, meetings) so the app can work and sync.
  • Kai, the AI coach, uses outside AI providers. With your permission, text you give Kai is sent to Anthropic, and meeting audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription. You can turn this off anytime in Settings → Kai & AI.
  • No ads. We never sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and name. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier and details Apple shares with us.
  • Your password, which is handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form.
  • Optional profile details you choose to add, such as an age group, plus your time zone and referral code.

Content you create

  • Habits, check-ins, streaks, tasks, sub-tasks and day-planner blocks.
  • Brain dumps (the text you type, or the transcript of what you say) and the tasks extracted from them.
  • Meetings: the title, transcript, summary, takeaways and action items. Meeting audio is recorded on your device and sent for transcription (see section 3); we don't keep the audio on our servers.
  • Messages you send to Kai in the chat are used to generate a reply; we don't save the conversation on our servers.

Social features

  • Friends, friend requests, accountability partners, challenges you join, leaderboard scores, XP and trophies.

Apple Health

  • If you give permission, Seika reads activity data such as steps, sleep and workouts from Apple Health to fill in your habits automatically, and may save your habit activity back to Apple Health. The values used to fill in a habit are saved with your habit history.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never sold. You can change these permissions anytime in the iPhone Settings app.

Calendar

  • If you choose to add a task to your calendar, Seika asks for Calendar access and creates that event on your device. Seika doesn't read or upload your existing calendar events.

Voice

  • When you speak a brain dump, your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's own privacy policy.

Device and technical information

  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • Crash reports (device model, iOS version, app version and what the app was doing when it crashed), linked to your account ID so we can fix problems.
  • Simple usage counts used to apply daily limits, such as how many Kai requests and minutes of transcription you've used.
  • Our service providers receive your IP address when the app connects to them.

Purchases

  • Seika Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive your subscription status, never your payment details.

Seika does not collect your precise location, contacts or photos.

2. How we use it

  • To run Seika's features and keep your data in sync across sign-ins.
  • To power Kai's coaching, task extraction, meeting summaries and reports (with your permission; see below).
  • To send the notifications you've turned on, including nudges from accountability partners and friends.
  • To apply fair-use limits, prevent abuse, fix crashes and provide support.
  • To send important service messages, such as changes to this policy. We don't send marketing emails unless you've asked for them.

3. Kai and AI services

Kai's AI features rely on two outside providers. Seika asks for your permission before sending anything to them, and you can turn AI features off or back on anytime in Settings → Kai & AI. With AI features off, Kai chat, Brain Dump task extraction and meeting notes won't work; everything else in Seika does.

ProviderWhat's sentWhen
Anthropic (Claude) Text you give Kai: brain dumps, chat messages, quick-add text and meeting transcripts, plus habit names and progress (which can include values from Apple Health) for tips and reports. Only when you use a Kai feature, or when Seika prepares a tip or report for you.
OpenAI (Whisper) The audio of meetings you record. Only when you record a meeting, to turn it into a transcript.

This data is sent through Seika's own server, never directly from your phone with our keys. The providers process it to return a result to Seika under their API terms. We never use it for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that run Seika for us:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions. Your data is stored in Canada.
    • Anthropic and OpenAI: AI features, only with your permission (section 3).
    • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports.
    • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, purchases, speech recognition, Apple Health and Calendar.
  • Other Seika users, depending on the features you use:
    • Your name and photo are visible to your friends, people in the same challenges as you, and people you've sent a friend request to.
    • Habits you haven't marked private, and your XP history, are visible to your friends.
    • Your name and weekly score can appear on leaderboards, and your trophies and level can be seen by signed-in users.
    • Other users can find you by name in friend search. Your full email address is only shown to someone who already typed it exactly.
  • If Seika is sold or transferred. If the app is sold, merged or transferred to a new owner, your information may be transferred as part of that deal. The new owner must continue to protect it in line with this policy, and we'll tell you in the app or by email before your information becomes subject to a different policy.
  • When required by law, or when we believe in good faith it's necessary to protect someone's safety, investigate fraud, or respond to a valid legal request.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), your account and the data linked to it are removed from our database. Copies may remain in routine backups for a limited time before those backups are overwritten. Crash reports are kept by Firebase according to its retention settings.

6. Your choices and rights

  • AI features: on or off in Settings → Kai & AI.
  • Notifications and Apple Health: manage them in Seika's Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Private habits: mark a habit private to hide it from friends.
  • Delete your account anytime from Settings → Delete Account.
  • Access, correct or export your data, or ask any privacy question, by emailing benschumacher15@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example, under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you may have additional rights, and we'll honour them.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each user's data is protected by access rules in our database so other users can only see what's described above, and the keys for our AI providers are kept on our server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects you as required by law.

8. Where your data is processed

Seika's database is hosted in Canada. Our AI and crash-reporting providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

9. Children

Seika is rated 13+ on the App Store and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you're under the age of digital consent where you live (16 in some countries), please use Seika only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For material changes, such as a new type of data shared with an AI provider, we'll let you know in the app and, where required, ask for your permission again.
