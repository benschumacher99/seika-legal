Meet Kai

A coach who knows when to be quiet.

Kai is the AI coach built into Seika. It helps where it's useful and stays out of the way the rest of the time — no daily essays, no fake hype.

  • Turns a rambling Brain Dump into a clean task list
  • Summarizes meetings into takeaways and action items
  • Nudges a streak that's at risk, celebrates real milestones
